`Pop.wav` is Apple's macOS `Pop.aiff`, converted to 16-bit little-endian PCM
without changing its sample rate, channels, timing, or gain. Copyright remains
with Apple; this asset is separate from the repository's software licenses.

Source: https://github.com/extratone/macOSsystemsounds/blob/main/aiff/Pop.aiff
Retrieved: 2026-10-24. macOS playback uses `/System/Library/Sounds/Pop.aiff`.
